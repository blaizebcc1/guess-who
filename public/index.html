How to play
  • Two players are paired. One guesser, one explainer (picked at random).
  • The explainer sees a secret word and describes it — over voice call and/or the chat.
  • The chat blocks any message that contains the word or a part of it.
  • Guesser hits Got it for a point, or Pass to skip (counts as a miss).
  • When the 2:00 timer ends, the word on screen is a miss. Then you review and swap roles.
  • Higher score across the two rounds wins.
